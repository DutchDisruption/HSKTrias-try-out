Here is how to organize your site structure cleanly, including your privacy policy:
1. Why Separate Pages Beat a Long One-Pager
Better AI & SEO indexing: Search engines and AI tools understand your business better when each topic (like Samples or Tech/CMS) has its own dedicated URL and clear heading structure.
Cleaner user experience: Visitors on mobile (like in your screenshot) do not have to scroll endlessly to find what they care about.

2. Recommended Page Setup
Keep your site lean with 3 to 4 main pages in your navigation menu:

Voorbeelden (Samples): Visual showcases, demo projects, and results.
Aanpak & Techniek (Tech & CMS): How your stack works, AI-readiness details, and how easy it is to edit content in the CMS.
Contact (or embedded form on every page): A simple way to request a quote or call.

3. Where to Put the Privacy Policy
Never put the Privacy Policy in your top menu. It takes up valuable space and distracts visitors.

Put it in the footer: Create a dedicated /privacy page and link to it cleanly at the very bottom of every page alongside your KvK number and contact details.

Why it is required: Under Dutch and EU GDPR (AVG) rules, you must have an accessible privacy policy if your site collects any visitor data—including contact form submissions, analytics, or cookies.
